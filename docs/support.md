# Help and support

If something in LEIA does not work as this documentation describes, or you cannot find how to do something, ask in **leia-users**, the public Telegram group where LEIA users and the LEIA team answer questions and collect problems.

**[Join leia-users on Telegram](https://t.me/leia_users)**

## Before you write

Many problems are already known and have a workaround. Look first at [Limitations and FAQ](teachers/faq.md), in particular its list of known problems, and at the [troubleshooting list of API keys and models](teachers/api-keys.md#troubleshooting).

## Report a problem in the group

1. Open [t.me/leia_users](https://t.me/leia_users) and join the group. You need a Telegram account, in the app or at [web.telegram.org](https://web.telegram.org).
2. Write one message per problem, with:
   - the application, **Designer** or **Workbench**, and the page or button where it happens;
   - what you did, step by step;
   - what you expected and what happened instead;
   - the exact error message, copied as text;
   - a screenshot, with private data hidden;
   - your browser, and the date and time it happened.
3. Keep the follow-up in replies to your first message, so each problem stays in one thread.

:::warning The group is public
Anyone can read the group. Never post API keys, passwords, the share link of a replication, which carries an access token, or the code of a replication that is active. Never post the names, email addresses or conversations of your students. Hide all of them in screenshots too. If you cannot describe the problem without them, write to [team@leia.ovh](mailto:team@leia.ovh) instead.
:::

## Accounts and publishing

Requests about your account go by email to [team@leia.ovh](mailto:team@leia.ovh), not to the group, because they need the email address of your account:

- the **advanced** role, needed for activities and the Workbench, see [The three roles](teachers/accounts-and-roles.md#the-three-roles);
- publishing one of your LEIAs so that other users can see it.

## Installing or developing LEIA

Questions about deploying your own LEIA also go to the group. Follow [Installation and deployment](installation/installation.md) first. Changes to the code go through pull requests, as described in [Contributing to LEIA](contributing/index.md).
