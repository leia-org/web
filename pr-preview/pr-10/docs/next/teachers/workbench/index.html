Workbench overview and your first replication

Who can do this

The advanced and admin roles. Instructors are refused at the Workbench sign-in with "Instructors cannot access workbench administration."

The Workbench is where activities meet students. You create a replication of a published activity, configure how it runs, share its code, follow the sessions and read the results. Students use the same site through a different door, the student login at the root of workbench.leia.ovh.

Sign in​

Open workbench.leia.ovh/login with the same email and password as the Designer.

#Field or buttonWhat it does
1EmailYour Designer account.
2PasswordYour password. The eye icon reveals it.
3VerificationThe automatic Cloudflare check. Sign in waits for it.
4Sign inOpens the replications list.

If you came from a link such as Replicate in the Designer, you are sent to that page after signing in.

Find your way around​

#ControlWhat it does
1SidebarReplications, Experiments and API Keys under Workspace, Logout under Admin.
2Search replicationsFilters the loaded list by name and code.
3Open filtersStatus (Active or Inactive), Repeatable or Single-run, Has time limit or Untimed, and the sort order, Name, Last updated (the default), Created, Code or Status.
4A replicationA green dot means students can join. The line shows the experiment, the time limit and whether it is repeatable, then the code and the time of the last change.
5Detail panelThe state, the code, the experiment, the duration, the repeatable flag and the dates.
6OpenThe replication workspace.
7LiveThe live dashboard of the selected replication.
8ConversationsThe results of the selected replication.

Administrators see every replication with the email of its owner. Advanced users see only their own, under the title My replications. The list panel can be widened by dragging its edge.

The list loads ten replications at a time

The list is sorted by last update and loads ten more replications each time you scroll to its bottom. The search box only filters the replications already loaded, so an old replication may not appear until you have scrolled far enough. On a tall window where ten replications fit without scrolling, the development version never loads the next ten. Make the window shorter, or open the replication from the Replicate button of its activity in the Designer.

New Replication at the top right goes to the experiments list.

Experiments​

Experiments are the activities published in the Designer. The Workbench does not edit them, it copies them into replications.

#ControlWhat it does
1An experimentThe name, the number of LEIAs and the modes they use. A green dot means published.
2Detail panelOwner, number of LEIAs, modes and dates.
3Create Replication from this ExperimentOpens the preview to confirm.

The filters offer Published or Unpublished and the modes found in the list. Unpublished activities appear but cannot be replicated.

Create a replication​

#ControlWhat it does
1PreviewThe activity as the Designer stored it, in JSON. Scroll it to check the LEIAs and the conversation flow before you replicate.
2Create ReplicationOpens the naming dialog.
3Go backReturns to the experiments list.

#ControlWhat it does
1Replication NameA unique name. It is prefilled with the name of the experiment. Add the group or the date, for example Library interview, group B, 14 October.
2CreateCreates the replication and opens its workspace.
3CancelCloses the dialog.

A replication starts inactive, not repeatable, untimed and in English. Its LEIAs are preconfigured with your default API key, but the model must be chosen and saved in every LEIA before the replication can be activated. See LEIA settings in a replication. Everything else is adjustable in the workspace.

The short way

Quick replication in the Designer library does all of this for a single LEIA in one click, including publishing an activity named after the LEIA. Use the Workbench route when an activity has several LEIAs or when you want several replications of the same activity.

The replication workspace​

Each replication has a sub-menu on the left.

SectionWhat you find therePage
GeneralThe student link, the code, the name, the language, the Active and Repeatable switches and the time limitReplication settings
LEIAsOne entry per LEIA with the solution and evaluation switches, the infographic options and the model, key and voiceLEIA settings in a replication
SettingsThe external form shown after the activityReplication settings
Data UsageConsent and automatic removal of conversationsReplication settings
ConversationsTranscripts, submissions, evaluations, scores and the CSV exportResults, grading and export
LiveSessions in progress, supervisor flags and spectate linksLive dashboard and spectating

Checklist before students arrive​

  1. Every LEIA has a Model and an API key saved in its Runner block. Without them the replication refuses to activate.
  2. Ask for student solution and Automatic evaluation are set the way you want for each LEIA.
  3. The Language is the language of your class.
  4. The Duration is set, or deliberately left without limit.
  5. You tried the activity with Test session or with the Demo / test link.
  6. Active is on.
  7. You copied the Student link or the code into your course page.
