LEIA Voice Mode

You can interact with the LEIA using your own voice! Just click the microphone button and start talking. LEIA will listen to your commands and respond accordingly, making it easier to use while coding or when your hands are busy. Try coding with it!

Students can interact with the LEIA in a deeply immersive way using natural language and submit their answers, which will be automatically graded with feedback provided.
